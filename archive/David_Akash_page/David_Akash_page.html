David Akash

David Akash has studied bamboo flute in India and with G. S. Sachdev at Ali Akbar College of Music. He has performed extensively in the US, Europe and Asia. He has been devoted to music of India for over twenty five years. David Akash has released two CDs: Song of the Swan and Waves of Bliss. Waves of Bliss features David Akash on bamboo flute and Anton Mizerak on keyboards and Tibetan bowls. It is available from Shastasong.

David Akash and Anton perform Classical Indian music regularly in Ashland, Oregon at Three Rivers Indian Resturant on Friday or Saturday nights from 6-8PM.. Check out Anton's tour page or phone Hardip at 541-482-0776.
