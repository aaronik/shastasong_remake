Richard Hardy

Richard Hardy a flute, saxophone and pennywhistle player who, after starting out his career with the Denver Symphony in the 1970s, recorded and performed with Carol King for thirteen years. Richard is featured on Anton's 1997 CD release, Desert Bloom, which topped out at #8 in the national New Age radio charts. Richard also plays two duets with Anton on Harmonica Sunset.

Richard is featured on many Imax film scores including Everest, Dolphins, Wild California, Journeys into Amazing Caves and Coral Reefs. His other recent releases are with Gringo Floyd, Shapeshifters, Third Force, Brian Hughes, Craig Chaquico and the Ultrakinetic Orchestra. Richard currently tours with Shakti Tribe.

Richard accompanies Anton on his southern California tours and on his annual summer solstice tour throughout Colorado.

Dreamscapes Anton Mizerak: keyboards & haarmonica; Richard Hardy: flute and soprano sax. Chuck Wilson: guitars.
