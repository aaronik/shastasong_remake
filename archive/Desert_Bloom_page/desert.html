Desert Bloom

by Anton Mizerak, Karl Joseph & Chuck Wilson

Anton Mizerak: keyboards harmonica and tabla.

Karl Joseph: nylon stringed guitars.

Chuck Wilson: steel stringed accoustic, electric and lap steel guitars.

Richard Hardy: flutes, soprano sax and pennywhistle.

Emam: dumbek.

Jim Hurley: guitar.

Cindy Titzer: vocals.

Sherril Kannasto: flute.

MP3 Downlaods:

Desert Bloom Anton MIzerak: keyboards; Karl Joseph: nylon stringed guitar. Chuck Wilson: electric and steel stringed guitar.

Harmonica Sunrise Anton Mizerak: keyboards & harmonica; Chuck Wilson: guitars; Karl Joseph: nylon stringed guitar.

Alpenglow Anton Mizerak: keyboards and harmonica; Karl Joseph: nylon stringed guitar. Chuck Wilson: electric and lap steel guitars; Richard Hardy: flute and soprano sax. Emam: dunbek.

More Desert Bloom MP3s are on Chuck Wilson and Karl Joseph page and Richard Hardy page.

"The plaintive voices of the harmonica and the lap steel guitar intertwined with Hispanic and Native accents conjured by the tabla, guitar and flute create a southwest flavor that really does invoke images of a desert in bloom. If you can't actually be out under the stars on a cool desert night near a fading camp fire, Desert Bloom will take you there." Carole Wright - NAPRA
