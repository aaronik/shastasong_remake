  Anton has lived in Mount Shasta for fifty years. He was custodian at Horse Camp, a Sierra Club climbers' cabin, in 1975. He has an MA in History of Religion from UCLA. He is a transformational healing music musician and member of the kirtan group "Parvati". A workshop leader for over ten years at the Spiritual Living Retreat at Asilomar, he has played music and facilitated fire circles for spiritual pilgrims to Mount Shasta for the past decade. In the 1970s he regularly attended the teaching of Pearl Dorris. He trained under two Native American medicine men. He has hosted spritual teachers at his Shastasong venue including the Dzog Chen teacher Lama Wangdor. He has worked for Mount Shasta retreats since 2023.

  LAURA BERRYHILL has three decades of experience connecting to the profoundly transforming power of Mount Shasta for personal insight and growth. As a professional musician and workshop leader, she has been privileged to experience the alchemy of transformation that comes from holding sacred space for others. Drawing daily inspiration from the beauty and subtlety of nature on and around Mount Shasta, her philosophy is to tread lightly on the path, nurturing our gifts of perception, attunement, and gratitude to allow us to experience the deepest wisdom our hearts and minds have to offer, and to receive.
