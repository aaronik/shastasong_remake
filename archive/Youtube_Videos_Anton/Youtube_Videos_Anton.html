Youtube Videos:

"Breathe" by Anton Mizerak. Whenever life looks hopeless, you can always breathe. First you will discover a deep peace within. Finally, you may understand the great discovery of the Upanishads: Tat tvam asi - That is you. The great light and spirit which you find outside of yourself is the same as the light and spirit that you discover on your inner journey. Anton MIzerak keyboards and vocals; Laura Berryhill vocals. Recorded at the Rogue Valley Center for Spiritual Living on July 5, 2026. Time 3:07.
"Bigger then Bigotry" by Anton Mizerak. After playing for the Mount Shasta Fourth of July celebrations for over forty years I decided to write a song about the kind of patriotism that inspires me. Anton Mizerak keyboards, vocals and harmonica; Laura Berryhill vocals; edited by Aurora Grace. Time: 3:24.
"Rise and Shine" words by Laura Berryhill and Anton Mizerak music by Anton Mizerak. A hopeful, cheery song to get us out of the funk of 2020. Performed by Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill at Rogue Valley Center for Spiritual Living on April 4, 2021. Time: 2:37.
"There's a Good for Me (and I Ought to Have It)" words by Emma Curtis Hopkins and Ernest Holmes; music by Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill. Aphorisms to guide us towards taking charge of our own lives. Anton MIzerak keyboards and vocals; Laura Berryhill penny whistle and vocals. Performed by Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill at Rogue Valley Center for Spiritual Living on April 4, 2021. Time: 3:04.
"Blarney Pilgrim" Anton Mizerak plays the Irish traditional "Blarney Pilgrim" in three different speeds on harmonica accompanied by Laura Berryhill on bodran at the Greater Las Vegas Center for Spiritual Living on Halloween 2011. The full studio version is available on the Harmonica Sunset CD. Time: 1:56.
"Autumn Rains" by Anton Mizerak. Composed at the Myrtle Fillmore Chapel at Unity Village in 2008. This live rendition performed at the Roque Valley Center for Spiritual Living in 2020 is shortened. I (Anton MIzerak) am playing keyboards; Laura Berryhill is playing penny whistle. The studio version is available on the Harmonica Sunset CD. Time: 1:31.
"We are the Crown of Creation" words and music by Anton Mizerak. Anton Mizerak keyboards and vocals; Laura Berryhill vocals. A song about the cosmic journey of our planetary development and our place in it. Recorded on September 19, 2021 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley. Time: 3:15.
"Clearing Meditation" with Kimberly Hawkins, Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill was recorded on August 29, 2021 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley. Kimberly Hawkins is the meditation guide. Live music by Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill. Anton Mizerak plays keyboards. Laura Barryhill plays Tibetan Bowls. Time: 1057.
"Ocean of Bliss" by Anton Mizerak. Anton Mizerak plays keyboards accompanied by Laura Berryhill on Tibetan Bowls at the Greater Las Vegas Center for Spiritual Living on Halloween 2011. The studio version is available on the CD "When Angels Dream 2". Time: 1:31.
