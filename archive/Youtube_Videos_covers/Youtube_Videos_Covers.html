"All I Ask of You" is forever to Remember Me as Loving You was written by Gregory Norbet of the Weston Priory in Vermont in the early 1970's. The Ishq allah (God is Love) chorus was added by Pir Valayat Kahn around 1974. This song is popular throughout Latin America so we have added the Spanish translation on the refrain after the chorus. Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals, Laura Berryhill on penny whistle and vocals. Recorded at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on January 23, 2022. Time 3:08.

"Light Your Lamp" performed by Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals and Laura Berryhill on penny whistle and vocals on October 3. 2021 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley. "Light Your Lamp and pass it on. Let it shine for everyone. So we all may know and wee all may see that we are one great family". I learned this song from Peter Spring who also played bamboo flute on my recording of Water Spirit from the CD "Unwind". Peter and I would close our concerts with this song, especially the annual four-day labyrinth celebrations in Ashland, Oregon which would culminate on new years eve. Time: 2:06.
