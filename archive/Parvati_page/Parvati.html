The musical group, Pàrvatì, consists of Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals, Laura Berryhill on vocals, and Alyssa Narum on vocals. This is part of the alap section.

Shiva Shambho, Parvati at Narayanhiti is a traditional Shiva kirtan arranged by Anton Mizerak and performed by the ecstatic kirtan group Parvati at Narayanhiti in Kathmandhu, Nepal. Parvati is Anton Mizerak on vocals and tabla, Alyssa Narum on vocals and harmonium and Laura Berryhill on vocals and manjira. Narayanhiti is a sacred area at the King's Palace consisting of a temple to Narayan (Vishnu) and a hiti: a sacred group of water spouts. The kirtan was part of an Arati (awakening of the God) on the eleventh day of the waxing moon: Shukla paksha ekadashi. March 2024.  
Maha Mantra at Narayanhiti Nepal by Parvati is a Krishna kirtan performed by the ecstaic kirtan group Parvati at Narayanhiti in Kathmandhu, Nepal. Parvati is Anton Mizerak on vocals and tabla, Alyssa Narum on vocals and harmonium and Laura Berryhill on vocals and manjira. Narayanhiti is a sacred area in the old Nepali King’s Palace where there is a temple to Narayan (Vishnu) and a hiti - a sacred group of water spouts. The melodic content is developed in Rag Yaman. The tabla tal alternates from rupak to kaharwa, finally ending in a fast (drut) tintal.
