"Auld Lang Syne by Robert Burns sung to the original melody with which it was published. Laura Berryhill and Anton Mizerak at 7700 feet in Avalanche Gulch just below Horse Camp. Recorded just before sunset around 4PM on New Years Eve of 2021. Video by Robert Webb who is also the caretaker for the Samoyed. At the very end of the video there is a group of trees making a line on the right side of Avalanche Gulch. In 1975 I read the entire Diamond Sutra (Vajracchedika Prajnaparamita) over the course of a summer at the base of one of those trees. Time: 0:38.
"Blarney Pilgrim" a traditional Irish folk tune recorded on my land on March 10, 2022 with Michael Mandrell on guitar, Laura Berryhill on Bodhran, and Anton Mizerak on Harmonica. Michael and I have been playing this tune for almost twenty years together. Laura and I for over twelve years together. 0:37
"Roosters in the Henhouse" composed by Anton Mizerak and recorded on the CD Harmonica Sunset. This version was recorded by Jade Webb in February 2022 on a volcanic dyke outcropping on the east side of Spring Hill. overlooking my house and recording studio and Mount Shasta. Anton Mizerak on harmonica and Laura Berryhill on manjira. Time 0:50.
"Rag Dhani at Panther Meadows" is a sawal jab featuring Nepali classical music maestro vocal Prabhu Raj Dhakal on vocals and Anton Mizerak on harmonica. Recorded by Laura Berryhill at upper Panther Meadows just below the spring on August 20. 2022. Time 0:59.
The Ecstatic Kirtan group Parvati, based in Mount Shasta, sing the Michael Stillwater classic "In the Light, In the Love, In the Glory" at Mossbrae Falls on January 10, 2025. As part of my Music in Nature series, I have sung appropriate songs at various Power Spots around Mount Shasta. This on was challenging. These waterfalls are vey loud, hence the sound editing to make the vocals pop out. There are no titles on this video as the software that I currently possess would degrade the video quality too much to justify added titles.. Time 0:54.
The Celestial Conjunction of Castor, Pollox and Mars as observed in the dark sky environment of Mount Shasta, CA, evokes images of the great synchronicity of life in the universe. The video was filmed by Rick Donnelley in the second week of January 2025. From top down, Castor, Pollox and Mars. Castor and Pollox being the twins from ancient Indo European Mythology: the Ashvins in ancient India, the Dioscuroi in ancient Greece, Gemini in ancient Rome. As clouds whizz by the stars and planet seem to sweep across the sky. Time 2:37.
