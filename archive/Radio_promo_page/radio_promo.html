Radio Promo Page

All Shastasong CDs have received extensive radio promotion.

Pacific Rim received airplay on over 400 stations throughout North America.

Unwind has been a favorite on New Age shows on public radio stations,

Desert Bloom topped out at #8 on the NAV national radio charts and received extensive play on Echoes.

When Angels Dream was on the NAV nation radio charts for 7 months.

When Angels Dream II is available for radio promo, programmers and music directors, please email or phone us and we will send you out a play copy.

For demos of Shastasong CDs please email us at anton@shastasong.com, tell us who you are, which CD you are interested in, what your station is, and what show you may use our music on. If you are new or we have never heard of you please include your mailing address and wattage of your station. Anton, Manose and Gary do radio interviews and we sometimes will do station ids for ou in our stdio.
