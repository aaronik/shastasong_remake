Chants and Hymns

"Amazing Grace" Anton Mizerak showcases his harmonica and down-home piano playing. This live rendition was performed at the Roque Valley Center for Spiritual Living in 2021 Anton Mizerak is playing keyboards, harmonica and singing; Laura Berryhill is singing. Time: 3:16.
"Good News, Chariot's A Comin'" Anton Mizerak and Laura Berryhill cover this Jubilee song from America's Black Church history. Recorded on September 19, 2021 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley. Time: 2:53.
"Stayed on Freedom" is based on an old black gospel hymn "Stayed on Spirit". It was reformulated in 1961 by Robert Wesby during the Freedom Rider movement. Performed by Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals and Laura Berryhill on vocals. Recorded at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on Februaryl 2022. Video production by Bill Miner. Live sound mixing by Tom Fredricks. Post production editing at Shastasong Studios in Mount Shasta, CA by Anton Mizerak. Time: 2:07.
"He Govinda" by Jagjit Singh. This bhajan is a cover of one of Jagjit Singh's beloved Krishna chants. Performed by Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals and Laura Berryhill on vocals. Recorded on January 23. 2022 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley in Medford, Oregon. Time 3:36
"Door of My Heart" by Paramahansa Yogananda. Yogananda brought the teachings of Kriya Yoga to America in the 1920, eventually settling in California. His book "Autobiography of a Yogi" has influenced many spiritual seekers for the past eight decades. Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals, Laura Berryhill on vocals. Performed at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley Februaary 27, 2022. Video production by Bill Miner. Live sound mixing by Tom Fredricks. Post production editing by Anton Mizerak at Shastasong Studios in Mount Shasta, CA. Time 2:53
The Lords Prayer by Albert Hay Malotte performed by Parvati. The three of us have been performing this piece separately for years. This is the first time that we all performed it together on August 13 at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley. The musical group, Pàrvatì, consists of Anton Mizerak on keyboards and vocals, Laura Berryhill on vocals, and Alyssa Narum on vocals.
"Oh Holy Night" is based on a French Poem by Placide Cappeau set to music by Adolphe Adam and rewritten in English by the Unitarian by minister John Sullivan Dwight who was influenced by the works of Ralph Waldo Emerson. This version from our 2025 Christmas concert series features Anton Mizerak, Laura Berryhill, Alyssa Narum and Mitchell Miller and was recorded at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on December 21, 2025. Time 4:32.
"Oh Come Oh Come Emmanuel" is an advent hymn sung in the week leading up to Christmas. Originally composed in Latin in the 8th or 9th Century, it was paired with the music that we know it by in 1851. Anton Mizerak has been playing an annual Christmas sing-along concert in Mount Shasta since the 1980's. This was the second year of Anton Mizerak, Laura Berryhill, Alyssa Narum and Mitchell Miller presenting the Mount Shasta Christmas sing-along. This video is from a second day performance in Medford, Oregon at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on December 21, 2025. Time 2:51.
"Go Tell It on the Mountain" is an African-American spiritual song and Christmas carol. This version for our 2025 Christmas concert series was recorded by Anton Mizerak, Laura Berryhill, Alyssa Narum and Mitchell Miller at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on December 21, 2025. Time 2:02.
"We Three Kings" written by John Henry Hopkins Jr.. This version from our 2025 Christmas concert series features Anton Mizerak, Laura Berryhill, Alyssa Narum and Mitchell Miller and was recorded at the Center for Spiritual Living Rogue Valley on December 21, 2025. Time 2:55.
